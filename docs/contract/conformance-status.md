# Conformance status

Audit of the implementation against [conformance.md](conformance.md). Updated as cases are closed.

Status legend:

- **PASS** — the behavior is implemented and covered by a test.
- **PARTIAL** — some parts pass, others are missing or blocked.
- **GAP** — specified in the contract but not implemented.
- **BLOCKED** — depends on email or another external piece that is not yet decided.

## Open gaps

C1 to C38 and C56 pass. C39 to C55, 17 cases, are gaps: they specify the guest booking widget and the self-service link of ADR-0017, whose contract is written and whose code is not. The five error codes they use (`online_booking_incomplete`, `online_booking_limit`, `self_service_link_invalid`, `self_service_closed`, `reservation_changed`) are in the registry in `apps/api/src/lib/errors.ts` so that C34's drift test holds, and no route emits them yet. Each row names the ADR-0017 stage that closes it.

## Notes

- Email delivery is Resend. The templates in `apps/api/src/lib/mailer.ts` are placeholders pending legal review of the text and basis; sends are gated by `notificationsEnabled` (default off).
